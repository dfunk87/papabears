Meet Kyle

Hello, I’m Kyle

Singing has always been part of my life. The youngest of four, forever trying to find my own voice. Could it get any more tragic?

But better than finding my voice was learning to use it: turning people’s exceptional days into perfect ones. And what’s more exceptional than your own special occasion?

I love working across genres: pop, R&B, swing, soul, musical theatre, folk, and more, so every performance can be completely tailored to you. I’ve been fortunate to sing in some incredible places, from Alexandra Palace to Abbey Road Studios, but the real honour is being invited to be part of your day.

So, will you give me the honour of making your exceptional day… perfect?

Questions

Questions couples ask

Can you sing at our ceremony and our evening party?

Yes. Book me for one moment or the whole day. The full-day package covers everything from your ceremony to the last dance.

Can we choose our songs, or say what not to play?

Yes to both. Send your must-plays, like your aisle song and first dance, plus any songs you never want to hear, and I’ll shape the rest of the set around them.

Do you bring your own sound system?

Yes. I bring a Bose L1 Pro16 PA, Shure SM58 and Beta 58A microphones and fully licensed backing tracks, so your venue doesn’t need to provide any sound equipment.

Are you insured?

Yes, I hold £10,000,000 public liability insurance, which most venues ask for before a performer can play.

Where do you travel?

I’m based in Luton and cover Bedfordshire, Hertfordshire and Buckinghamshire. I’m happy to travel further when travel and accommodation are covered.

How do we secure our date?

Once you’re happy with your quote, a 10% deposit holds the date for you.
