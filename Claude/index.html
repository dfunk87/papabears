About

Liner notes

Singing has always been part of my life. The youngest of four, forever trying to find my own voice. Could it get any more tragic?

But better than finding my voice was learning to use it: turning people’s exceptional days into perfect ones. And what’s more exceptional than your own special occasion?

I love working across genres: pop, R&B, swing, soul, musical theatre, folk, and more, so every performance can be completely tailored to you. I’ve been fortunate to sing in some incredible places, from Alexandra Palace to Abbey Road Studios, but the real honour is being invited to be part of your day.

Whether that’s twenty minutes or two hours, I’d love to help bring your songs to life.

So, will you give me the honour of making your exceptional day… perfect?

How it works

The small print

  1. Tell me your date

    Send a quick enquiry with your date, venue and the moments you’d like music for.

  2. Hear back in 24 hours

    Availability and a clear quote, no chasing.

  3. Secure your date

    A 10% deposit holds your booking.

  4. Shape your set

    Send your must-plays and never-plays, and I’ll build the rest.

Do you bring your own sound system?

Yes. I bring a Bose L1 Pro16 PA, Shure SM58 and Beta 58A microphones and fully licensed backing tracks, so the venue doesn’t need to provide any sound equipment.

Are you insured?

Yes, I hold £10,000,000 public liability insurance, which most venues ask for before a performer can play.

Where do you travel?

I’m based in Luton and cover Bedfordshire, Hertfordshire and Buckinghamshire. I’m happy to travel anywhere in the UK when travel and accommodation are covered.

What styles do you sing?

Jazz, swing, pop, soul, R&B, Motown, musical theatre and folk. Most sets mix a few of these so every generation in the room has something to sing along to.

Can I request songs, or tell you what not to play?

Yes to both. Send your must-plays and any songs you never want to hear, and I’ll shape the rest of the set around them.

How do I secure my date?

Once you’re happy with your quote, a 10% deposit holds the date for you.
